MODEL AUDIT v0.2 / 2026-10-17
Audited means the stated scope below, not every paper's equations.

M0 Effective Newtonian quantum-matter interaction
State: two branch qubits. Hamiltonian: diagonal -G m_A m_B/r. Quantized matter.
Reproduced static phase/entanglement with environment modeled separately.
The matter-only effective Hamiltonian does not establish the mediator ontology.
No field dynamics, relativistic corrections, moving packets or apparatus included.

M1 Kafri–Taylor–Milburn measurement-and-feedback classical channel
Reproduced static branch restriction of S02 Eq.16. Classical-communication model
cannot entangle a separable input under the stated assumptions. It is a benchmark,
not our strongest viable contender. S21 constrains pairwise extensions using atom
interferometry; do not generalize those exclusions to all classical-quantum models.

M2 Mean-field semiclassical / Schrödinger–Newton
Specific state-dependent field dynamics; not one universal classical model.
S10's optical cross-correlation proposal is identified, not reproduced. Conditioning
and preparation assumptions must be fixed before comparing measurement records.

M3 Oppenheim stochastic classical-quantum framework
A family of completely-positive stochastic hybrid dynamics, not one fixed noise
curve. S03 and S09 motivate joint diffusion/decoherence tests. Not implemented.
Need select an explicit realization and parameter priors independently of data.

M4 Gaussian DP dynamics analyzed by Trillo–Navascués
Selected static equal-mass equations, roots and entangling example reproduced.
Classification: mathematical entanglement demonstrated within model; status as
local classical mediation DISPUTED; empirical viability of our parameters OPEN.
Finite bodies, smearing scale, heating, shielding and apparatus coherence not
established. S08 addresses locality; S20 argues actual table-top constraints alter
the inference. Do not call it a proven viable counterexample to the local-mediator
theorem, or replace its correlated noise with independent dephasing.

M5 Aziz–Howl classical gravity with quantum matter-field propagation
Nature paper S04 makes an entanglement claim. S14, S15 and S22 challenge dynamics,
subsystem partition, and/or whether quantized matter exchange is the mediator.
Our initial charter overstated this as a settled demonstration. Corrected to
DISPUTED. No full QFT derivation has yet been independently reproduced here.
A supplied definition of classical field alone does not settle locality, allowed
non-gravitational channels, identical-particle partitions or postselection.

THE INFERENCE WE WILL ACTUALLY TEST
Observed entanglement + verified initial separability + exclusion of other quantum
channels + an applicable local-mediator theorem can support nonclassical gravity.
The theorem is conditional; a countermodel must violate a conclusion while meeting
its assumptions, not merely attach the word classical to different dynamics.

EXPERIMENT SELECTION
Candidate A, mass-branch/spin readout: computational benchmark exists; apparatus
and viable-alternative audit remains open. Candidate B, diffusion/coherence: source
screening only. Candidate C, mirror cross-correlations: source screening only.
Do not select A as the best experiment simply because its toy code is easiest.
A future scorecard must compare the same model parameters, constraints, data
likelihoods and nuisance treatment. Three candidates remain in consideration.
